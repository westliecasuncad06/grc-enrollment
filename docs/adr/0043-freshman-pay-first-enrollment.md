# ADR 0043 — Freshmen Pay First: Queue Number at Admission, Account and Automatic Enrollment After Payment

**Status:** Accepted and implemented (Slice 2: queue number at Admission; Slice 3: payment confirmation, automatic enrollment, waiting path, notifications and the Registrar view — see "How Slice 3 works" below)
**Date:** 2026-10-04
**Amends:** ADR 0030 (Registrar approval for every enrollment, now not for freshmen), ADR 0041 (what Admission does when it creates a freshman), ADR 0023 and 0027 (the Cashier queue only held enrollments)
**Related:** ADR 0044 (the public live-queue link), ADR 0042 (student numbers), ADR 0025 (Payee/Scholar), ADR 0028 (promotion and back subjects)

**Amended 2026-10-15 (ADR 0050):** a Transferee gets the queue number when no credit is left `pending` (the Program Chair has passed the credits on), not when the Registrar Head has decided the last one.

## Context

Until now Admission created a freshman's account and immediately emailed the account setup code. The freshman could not reach the Cashier before logging in, submitting an enrollment, and getting the Registrar's approval. The stakeholder revision ("Changes sa system for revision", 2026-10-04) reverses that order for a **freshman**: they pay first, and the account and the enrollment follow the payment.

## Decisions

1. **Admission no longer sends the account setup email to a freshman.** Instead it issues a **queue number** from the same Cashier queue as everyone else and emails it, with a link to a live view of the queue (ADR 0044). The number is shown on the Admission screen as well, so a failed email does not stop the freshman. The student and the queue number are recorded in **one transaction** (`AdmitStudent`), with the emails sent only after it commits: a failure issuing the number leaves no account behind, so the same email can simply be tried again (an earlier version committed the account first, and a failure at the queue step left a student with no number whose email was then refused as "already taken").
2. **The account is created after the Cashier confirms the first payment.** The `users` row (disabled, with a random password) and the `student_profiles` row still exist from Admission — that is the smallest change and keeps the student number and the requirements checklist — but the freshman cannot do anything with them: the setup code is sent only after payment (`SendStudentAccountSetupInvitation` refuses an unpaid freshman, which covers the Admission resend, the Super Admin resend and the public resend). From the student's side the account appears after payment.
3. **A ticket may belong to a student profile instead of an enrollment.** `queue_tickets.enrollment_id` is nullable, `student_profile_id` is a unique foreign key, and a CHECK requires at least one. A freshman's ticket draws from the same open `queue_cycles` row and `Q%03d` sequence, so a freshman waits in the same line. A cycle is not considered drained while an unpaid freshman's ticket is waiting, so numbering does not reset to Q001 under them.
4. **Idempotency key:** `student_profiles.freshman_payment_confirmed_at`, set once when the first payment is confirmed. It gates the setup email and makes the confirmation safe to repeat.
5. **The Cashier sees whether a ticket is a freshman's** (`is_freshman` on the queue ticket, shown as a "Freshman" badge).
6. **Scope:** only `student_type = freshman`. Transferee, Returnee and Existing Student keep the previous flow (account setup email at Admission, enrollment through the portal).

### Decided with the owner for Slice 3

7. **On the Cashier's confirmation the freshman is enrolled automatically in the first section with open seats** of their program and year (IT101, and IT102 when IT101 is full), with **no Registrar approval**. Registrar Staff and the Registrar Head get a read-only list of the freshmen enrolled this way.
8. **Payment rule for a freshman: at least PHP 1,000, no promissory note.** The rule for every other student is unchanged (PHP 1,000 minimum on the first payment; a promissory note when it is below the assessment). The unpaid balance stays on the student's account.
9. **If every section is full, the payment is still accepted** and held as credit; the freshman gets their account, is notified when a section opens, enrolls in it on the Enrollment page and Registrar Staff approve it; that approval completes the enrollment without a second payment.
10. **The password is set from the existing six-digit setup email**, sent after payment.

## How Slice 3 works

- **Confirm:** `POST /student-profiles/{id}/freshman-payment` (Accounting Staff) runs `ConfirmFreshmanPayment` in one transaction under the profile lock. It checks `freshman_payment_confirmed_at` first (a repeat is a 200 that changes nothing), validates the amount (`FreshmanPaymentRules`: at least `enrollment.freshman_payment.minimum_amount`, default 1000.00), then either enrolls or holds.
- **Choice of section:** `BuildEnrollmentBlockPool` with the Year-1 enrollment window ignored (the Cashier drives this path), sorted by natural block number, first block that is selectable with a seat in every subject. Only for `enrollment_category = regular`; an Irregular-category freshman is not put in a block and gets credit only (they are told to choose their subjects on the Enrollment page, not that every section is full, and they are not sent the "a section opened" notice). `SubmitEnrollment` re-checks the seats under the section lock; a block that filled in between is skipped, and if none is left the payment is held.
- **Enrolled path:** `SubmitEnrollment` (no Registrar/Program Chair fan-out), the new `freshman_auto_approve` transition in `TransitionEnrollment` (assessed, no Registrar actor, no `registrar_decided_at`; the audit row says so), then `ConfirmPayment(freshmanFirstPayment: true)`, which lifts only the promissory-note rule. The queue ticket is linked to the enrollment and served.
- **Held path:** the money is recorded as a null-enrollment `AccountPayment` (the same advance-credit ledger the Cashier already uses), the ticket is served, and the freshman is told they are waiting for a section. `NotifyAwaitingFreshmen` runs when a section is published (`TransitionScheduleProposal`) or a published section gains capacity (`UpdateSection`), once per freshman per change, for freshmen of that program and year only. The email is a courtesy and never fails the section change.
- **Completion of a held payment:** when Registrar Staff approve the freshman's own submission, `SettleEnrollmentFromAdvanceCredit` re-points the held credit rows to the enrollment and calls `ConfirmPayment` with an amount of 0.00, as the cashier who took the money. The PHP 0.00 `Payment` row is the idempotency anchor (`payments.enrollment_id` is unique), so a repeat finds no held rows and nothing is counted twice.
- **What the waiting student is told:** the profile carries `advance_payment_held` (true while the held credit has not been applied). While it is true and the enrollment is not yet enrolled, the Enrollment page says the payment is already on file and that approval enrolls them with no queue number and nothing more to pay, instead of the usual "claim your queuing ticket" guidance; it is derived from the money, not the term, so a later semester's enrollment of the same student is unaffected.
- **After commit:** the account setup invitation (both paths) and the enrollment confirmation email (enrolled path, and after a settled approval).
- **Registrar view:** `GET /freshman-enrollees` for Registrar Staff and the Registrar Head, read-only, paginated, filterable by status (`enrolled`, `enrolling`, `awaiting_section`, `awaiting_payment`) and searchable. `student_profiles` also exposes `freshman_status` for the student's own Enrollment page banner.
- **Not affected:** schedule generation and section planning never write enrollments or `enrolled_count`.

## Consequences

- Two migrations to run on local and on Hostinger: `2026_10_04_000002_allow_queue_tickets_without_an_enrollment` and `2026_10_04_000003_add_freshman_payment_confirmed_at_to_student_profiles_table`. They are not applied to any shared database by the code change.
- A browser still running the older frontend would not understand the new fields; the frontend schemas default them so an older backend still parses (and the newer frontend against an older backend simply shows no queue number).
- A freshman who is waiting for a section submits through the ordinary Enrollment page, so the normal enrollment-window rules still apply to that submission (only the Cashier path ignores the window).
- The Cashier's "Find student" candidate search was not extended to freshmen: a freshman is reached through the queue (Now serving), which is how the stakeholder described the flow.
- Not decided here: a refund or void path for a freshman payment after automatic enrollment; whether the queue number should expire if the freshman never comes.

## Amendment 2026-10-04 — the Registrar approves before enrollment

The owner reversed the "auto-enrolled, no Registrar approval" rule above. Their report: the freshman's enrollment did not appear in Registrar → Enrollment Approvals, the student showed "Enrolled" before the Registrar had acted, and the enrolled email went out at payment. The rule now is: the Cashier takes the payment; the freshman is submitted to the first block with open seats; the enrollment waits in Registrar Staff's approvals queue; only the Registrar's approval makes them enrolled and sends the enrolled email (with the COR).

- **What changed in the code:** `ConfirmFreshmanPayment` holds the payment as credit (an `account_payments` row with no enrollment) before the enrollment exists, submits the block enrollment (`pending_registrar_approval`), stamps `enrollments.payment_confirmed_at`, and does not call `ConfirmPayment` or the `freshman_auto_approve` transition (removed from `TransitionEnrollment`). Its outcome is `awaiting_registrar_approval` (or `awaiting_section` when no block could take them). The Registrar is notified (`freshman_awaiting_registrar_approval`). The Cashier's request sends only the account-setup email. The approval is `registrar_approve`, which assesses, then `SettleEnrollmentFromAdvanceCredit` applies the held credit: Enrolled, the COR, and one enrolled email from `EnrollmentController::update`. `ConfirmPayment` keeps the Cashier's `payment_confirmed_at` rather than overwriting it.
- **Unchanged:** no schema change and no migration; idempotency on `freshman_payment_confirmed_at`; the PHP 1,000 minimum; no promissory note.
- **Still open, owner's question:** "this setup is for freshmen only and transferee". Transferees have no pay-first queue in the code, so the change stays freshman-only until the owner says otherwise.

## Amendment 2026-10-05 — transferees pay first too

The owner answered the open question above: "freshmen only and transferee" means **transferees pay first as well**. The design is in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-04-transferee-credit-mapping-pay-first-design.md` (Slice 3) and the plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-05-transferee-queue-number-pay-first-and-prospectus.md`. Freshmen are unchanged.

- **Who pays first:** a Freshman and a Transferee (`StudentType::isPayFirst`). A Returnee and an Existing Student follow the ordinary flow.
- **Queue number:** a transferee gets their number when their credit mapping has nothing left open (at least one submitted credit, none `pending` or `endorsed`; `IssueTransfereeQueueTicket`, called inside every step that can close the last row: the Registrar Head's approve or reject, the Program Chair's decline, approve-all). It is the same profile-linked ticket, from the same open cycle and `Q%03d` sequence, issued once, and emailed after commit with the no-login live link (ADR 0044). A failed email never undoes the ticket. Unlike a freshman, a transferee has a working account and signs in before paying to choose their subjects.
- **The payment hold:** a transferee's submitted enrollment waits at the new non-terminal status `awaiting_first_payment` instead of joining the Registrar's queue (a regular submission, or after the Program Chair approves an Irregular one, ADR 0030, or the student accepts the Chair's changes, ADR 0040; `EnrollmentRouting::statusAfterReview`). The hold reserves the seats, the student may cancel and the Registrar may void it, and the Registrar never sees it. A transferee who already paid (the money was held before they chose) goes straight to the Registrar and settles from the held credit. The status needs no migration (`active_academic_term_id` is NULL only for rejected, cancelled and withdrawn).
- **The Cashier:** the same endpoints (`freshman-payment-preview`, `freshman-payment`) accept a transferee. The payment is held as credit first; the enrollment moves from `awaiting_first_payment` to `pending_registrar_approval`; the ticket is served; Registrar Staff and the Registrar Head are told; no one is enrolled and no enrolled email is sent. A transferee with no enrollment yet is told to choose and submit subjects. No account-setup email (they already have an account). Registrar approval settles from the held credit (`SettleEnrollmentFromAdvanceCredit`), as for a freshman.
- **Idempotency key:** `student_profiles.freshman_payment_confirmed_at` is reused for both types. The column name says "freshman" for a transferee (cosmetic); a rename is a later migration. The endpoint, notification and audit names also keep "freshman".
- **Standing:** approving credits re-derives Regular or Irregular for the student (`ReclassifyAfterCreditApproval`), because a credited subject counts as completed. The Prospectus already shows the previous school's grade for approved credits, as information only (PRD §17 stays open).
- **Unchanged:** the PHP 1,000 minimum, no promissory note, no migration.
- **Not decided here:** an expiry for a hold or an unused number (a hold keeps its seats until the student cancels, the Registrar voids it or the term ends); a refund or void of the payment after it is taken; Returnee pay-first.
